💡 How BidShield AI Works

End-to-end procurement intelligence — from document upload to verified decision support

Our Solution — In Our Own Words
"Sir, humara solution basically government procurement ko faster, transparent aur data-driven banane ke liye hai. Currently government tenders mein bidder ke GST, Udyam, registration, financial aur other eligibility documents ko manually verify karna padta hai, jisme kaafi time lagta hai aur human error ka scope bhi hota hai.

Hum ek AI-powered Bidder Verification Platform bana rahe hain, jahan bidder apna profile create karke required documents upload karega. AI automatically documents ko analyse karke missing documents, validity, consistency aur eligibility requirements check karega aur ek Compliance Score generate karega.

Lekin hum sirf documents tak limited nahi hain — hum bidder ke past performance ko bhi track karenge. Government inspection teams project sites se geo-tagged photos aur videos upload kar sakengi. Applicable cases mein citizens bhi evidence submit kar sakenge.

Hamare solution ka second major component AI Agent hai. Agar kisi bidder ka compliance score low hai, AI agent usko batayega ki problem exactly kaha hai aur score/readiness improve karne ke liye kya documents ya actions required hain."
— Team BidShield, SIH 2024

📤 Bidder Journey

STEP 01
🏢
Bidder Registration
Bidder creates profile with GSTIN, PAN, CIN, Udyam number. System fetches live data from government APIs immediately.
STEP 02
📄
Document Upload
Upload GST certificates, financial statements, registration proofs, OEM authorizations. AI performs OCR and cross-validates.
STEP 03
📊
Compliance Score Generated
Weighted score across eligibility, blacklist check, document validity, financial capability, and past performance.

🏛️ Officer / Decision Flow

STEP 01
🔍
Officer Reviews Dashboard
Single unified view of all bidders for a tender with scores, risk flags, document status, and past performance.
STEP 02
🤖
AI Verification Runs
One click triggers 7 parallel API connectors — GSTN, MCA21, Udyam, NIC Blacklist, CIBIL, GeM, eProcurement.
STEP 03
⚡
Informed Decision Made
Officer gets AI-structured intelligence — not just data, but actionable insights. Approve, reject, or request clarification.

📍 Performance Tracking & Citizen Participation

FEATURE 01
📸
Site Inspection Evidence
Government inspection teams upload geo-tagged photos & videos from project sites. Timestamped, tamper-proof, auto-linked to bidder profile.
FEATURE 02
👥
Citizen Evidence Portal
Citizens can submit evidence about contractor work quality. Verified submissions are added to the bidder's performance record.
FEATURE 03
🧠
AI Compliance Agent
Low-score bidders get personalised AI guidance on exactly which documents are missing and what steps to take to improve readiness.

Our Goal
We are not replacing the procurement officer. We are giving the officer verified, structured and actionable intelligence to make better decisions — faster. The platform serves as a verification and decision-support system for government, and an AI-powered compliance assistant for bidders.
